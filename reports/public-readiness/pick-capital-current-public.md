## Reproduced defects and correction

Actual `dedbb16` module fixtures reproduced these substantive calculation/context defects:

- **High: seasonal future capital.** A 16-round redraft league received 48 picks in 2026–2028. Redraft, keeper and supported seasonal formats now count only remaining current-season rights; no dynasty future book or keeper-value economics is inferred. Numeric zero, blank fields and the current canonical format override store are respected.
- **High: foreign draft context.** A complete draft belonging to another league retired the target league's current-year picks. A global draft list now requires the matching selected league and, when present, matching `draftsLeagueId`; individual rows must also belong to that league. Explicit league arrays take precedence. Historical wrappers use the selected league's actual `S.season`, not the computer's current year.
- **High: consumed or uncertain rights.** A partially drafted, transferred slot remained tradeable. A made Sleeper pick consumes its **original slot owner** from `slot_to_roster_id`, not the current recipient's different original pick. MFL's seeded board uses its explicit current slot owners; unknown original owners remain null. Missing progress, conflicting/unresolved ownership, absent round evidence and ambiguous multiple current boards produce an unavailable assessment, not full capital or a deficit. Current startup rounds can differ from future rookie rounds. Explicit zero rounds does not turn into a five-round fallback.
- **High: stale memo and saved assessment.** Same-length ownership changes and draft completion did not invalidate the memo or daily pin. Compact canonical fingerprints now include format, league/season, rounds, owned draft progress and ownership. Input reordering alone retains stability. Pin revision advances from 6 to 7, and late cloud responses cannot overwrite another league or changed pick state.

The provider documents the original-owner field for traded picks and separates a draft pick's current roster from its board-slot mapping. Those are the identity contracts used here; no current owner is guessed from a drafted player's recipient. [Sleeper draft and traded-pick documentation](https://docs.sleeper.com/#drafts)

The existing completed-dynasty contract is preserved: four rounds with every 2026 draft complete yields **12 picks in 2027/2028/2029**. No player-value file, rookie/pick pricing engine, one-brain overlay, injury handling, quality floor, GM strategy or rank/health formula changed. Actual baseline-versus-candidate assessment comparison matches all non-pick fields for the protected fixture.

## Independent review follow-up: complete board evidence

Root identified a material remaining defect in `4880edd`: the MFL fixture supplied only two first-round rows for a configured four-round/two-roster board, but the helper called that complete. The exact negative fixture now fails against `4880edd` and passes after correction. A legitimate one-round current draft is still supported when its settings explicitly establish that board; the original positive fixture now states that requirement.

The actual `mfl-api.js:mapDraftStatus` derives `settings.rounds` from the maximum returned round and `settings.teams` from returned row count divided by rounds. Those values describe the response rather than independently proving its completeness. The mapper now adds `_dimensionsInferred: true`; the assessment then requires separate league configuration instead of letting a truncated response certify itself. Every expected round/slot must occur exactly once, with valid owner and matching optional league/draft identity. A truncated all-made board cannot roll the year forward. Valid complete MFL and Sleeper dynasty boards still retain 2027–2029. Arbitrary custom or variable-length boards without an independently established shape remain unavailable; no unverified MFL configuration or default round count is invented. The current public MFL mapper does not supply independent `draft_rounds`, so that provider's full capital assessment remains an explicit integration dependency.

Sleeper drafting evidence likewise requires a complete, unique original slot-owner map, rejects duplicate consumed positions and foreign league/draft/roster records, and continues to consume the original slot after a transfer. Evidence changes invalidate both the live memo and saved pin; same-size board repairs, missing player-field recovery, changed board dimensions and reload are exercised under both ready and not-yet-ready intelligence state.
